# Art credits

The following game art is bundled under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
public-domain dedication:

- Boss artwork: **12 Public Domain Boss Sprites!** by titleknown:
  https://opengameart.org/content/12-public-domain-boss-sprites
- Weapons and magic items: **Wesnoth items** by Blarumyrran:
  https://opengameart.org/content/wesnoth-items
- Armour pieces: **Armor Icons by Equipment Slot** by Clint Bellanger,
  Blarumyrran, crowline, and Justin Nichol:
  https://opengameart.org/content/armor-icons-by-equipment-slot
- Painted high-tier equipment: **Fantasy RPG Icons** by drummyfish:
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-rpg-icons-0
- Sprite weapons, staves, shields and scale armour: **Dungeon Crawl 32x32
  tiles** by the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup team (Chris Hamons and contributors):
  https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles — the CC0
  dedication ships in `LICENSE.txt` inside that release.

The original art was cropped, resized, or framed for use in Fun-Work. Sprite
pieces are trimmed to their drawn pixels, scaled by a whole number so they stay
crisp, and centred on a fixed canvas so every armory card frames alike. No
attribution is required by CC0, but the creators and source pages are recorded
here with thanks.

Everything is bundled in `public/assets/` and precached by the service worker,
so the armory works with no network.
